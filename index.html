💡 World Tipping Customs

🇯🇵 Japan / South Korea: No tipping. Good service is standard; leaving cash can be considered rude.
🇪🇺 Europe (France, Italy, Spain): Service charge often included. 5–10% or round up for great service.
🇬🇧 United Kingdom: 10–12.5% in sit-down restaurants (check bill for optional service charge).
🇲🇽 Mexico: 10–15% in restaurants (called "propina").
🇺🇸 USA / Canada: 18–22% expected for dining and bartenders.
